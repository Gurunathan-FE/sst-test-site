About us

A small shop with a big love for two wheels.

Velo Foundry started as a workshop bench and a few favourite frames. Today we are a full-service bike shop: sales, hire, repairs, and the kind of chat you only get from people who actually ride.

We believe buying a bike should be straightforward. No pressure, clear options, and time to try things properly. If we do not stock what you need, we will say so—and point you somewhere that does.

What we care about

Safety, fit, and long-term value. We would rather sell you the right bike once than the wrong bike twice.

Community rides and skills sessions run seasonally; ask in store or via our contact page for dates.
